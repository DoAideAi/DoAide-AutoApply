Privacy Policy

DoAide AutoApply (formerly TalentPing) — Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Who We Are

DoAide AutoApply (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an AI-powered job-application automation service available at job.doaide.com. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our service.

2. Data We Collect

When you sign up and use DoAide AutoApply, we may collect the following:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and Google account identifier used to sign in.
  • Gmail data: when you connect a mailbox through Google OAuth, we receive its email address, Google account identifier, authorization tokens, and granted permissions. We request gmail.send to send messages and gmail.readonly to read messages. We access message and thread identifiers, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, timestamps, message bodies, and attachments needed for job correspondence. Background mailbox searches may inspect messages outside existing application threads to identify inbound recruiter opportunities; access is not technically restricted to known recruiters.
  • Resume and profile data: resumes, cover letters, professional profiles, and job preferences you upload or provide.
  • Job application data: records of jobs you apply to, application status, recruiter responses, and follow-up correspondence.
  • Usage data: feature usage, page views, and interactions within the application for service improvement.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Job correspondence: we send application emails, follow-ups, and replies; identify inbound recruiter messages; classify responses; and display correspondence and attachments in your application inbox. Depending on the automation settings you enable, messages may be sent automatically rather than individually approved. You can pause automation or disconnect a mailbox. Read-only access does not let us delete messages, change labels, or change their read/unread state.
  • AI assistance: resume, profile, and job-posting information is processed to generate application materials. External AI email assistance is optional and off until you affirmatively opt in under Email AI privacy in Setup. For email classification, outreach, follow-ups, and reply drafting, selected sender/recruiter information, subjects, message-body excerpts, and relevant profile or resume context may then be processed by eligible AI endpoints. You can withdraw consent in the same place. Without current consent or an eligible endpoint, local rules and templates are used.
  • Application management: we store application records to provide you with a dashboard showing statuses, responses, and follow-up actions.
  • Service improvement: aggregated, de-identified usage data helps us improve features and fix issues.

4. Gmail API — Limited Use Disclosure

The use of raw or derived information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This commitment is not a statement that Google has verified or approved the application:

  • Gmail access is used for the job-correspondence, inbox, classification, and reply features described above, not unrelated profiling.
  • We do not use Gmail data for advertising purposes.
  • We do not allow humans to read your email content unless you give explicit consent, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.
  • Transfers of Gmail data for user-facing features require your consent. Other permitted transfers are limited to security purposes, legal requirements, or a business transfer with your explicit prior consent. These restrictions also apply to data derived from Gmail.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • AI service providers: Gmail email assistance uses Groq inference with verified zero-retention settings, or OpenRouter and its eligible model providers, only after updated, recorded consent. Groq requests use only its standard chat-completions endpoint and approved base models, not batch, file storage, fine-tuning, LoRA, or built-in external tools. An operator's zero-retention verification is bound to the exact API key and expires after 30 days; changed keys, endpoints, or unapproved models block this route. Groq inference zero-retention disables storage of inputs and outputs for reliability and abuse monitoring; non-content usage metadata remains. OpenRouter requests require data_collection: deny and zdr: true routing. Neither route is used to train generalized AI models. Direct Gemini and Cerebras fallback remains prohibited for Gmail data. If no eligible provider is available, processing remains local. Gmail OAuth credentials are never in AI prompts. OpenRouter retains operational request metadata and permits temporary in-memory prompt caching, so zero retention is not a promise that no data is ever held during processing. Separate non-email features can use configured AI APIs for the resume, profile, or job-posting information you provide; email processing is isolated from that fallback chain.
  • Infrastructure and delivery: application data is stored on our hosted servers and database. Google processes OAuth authorization and Gmail API requests. When we send an email you authorize, its recipients receive the message and any attachments.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • With your consent: for any other purpose, only with your explicit permission.

6. Data Retention

  • Your account data and application records are retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Account deletion removes your user-linked records from the live database, including resumes, application history, stored correspondence, and encrypted Gmail tokens. The deletion process also attempts to remove associated uploaded resume files and application screenshots and revoke Google authorization.
  • Disconnecting a mailbox removes its stored credentials and attempts to revoke its authorization; it does not delete application history or messages already sent to recipients. You can also revoke access directly in your Google Account.
  • Managed AutoApply database backups are encrypted and retained for up to 30 days, with expired copies removed by the daily backup job. Historical AutoApply code and database backups converted to encrypted archives on October 14, 2026 are also subject to that 30-day archive retention. Backups are not used for ongoing application processing. Contact us to review backup copies or any file-deletion failures. Deleting your account does not erase copies held by email recipients or external providers.
  • De-identified, aggregated data may be retained for analytics purposes.

7. Data Security

Public application traffic uses HTTPS. AutoApply's database, Redis queues, uploaded artifacts, and scheduler state are stored on an isolated LUKS-encrypted volume. Server swap is encrypted with a fresh key at each boot. Managed AutoApply backups are separately encrypted and restore-tested. Stored Gmail OAuth tokens additionally use Fernet encryption and are decrypted on the backend only when needed to call Google's services. Application services require the encrypted storage to be mounted and do not fall back to plaintext data storage. Unlock keys are restricted to the server administrator, with a protected recovery copy off-server; this is not hardware-backed key management and does not protect against a compromised administrator or a running-server compromise. The operating-system volume and historical system logs are not covered by this application-storage encryption. We suppress OAuth callback access logs and avoid logging OAuth provider response bodies. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data.
  • Revoke Gmail access at any time through your Google Account permissions.
  • Export your application data.

9. Browser Storage

The application uses browser local storage for its sign-in session token. Google may use its own cookies during authorization on Google pages. We do not use Gmail data for advertising or advertising-cookie targeting.

For new access to or new uses of Google user data beyond what you previously authorized, we must notify you and obtain your consent before that access or use. Continued use alone is not consent to a new Google-data use.
